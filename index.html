About me

Engineering infrastructure that builds, runs, and heals itself

I'm a DevOps and Cloud Infrastructure professional with 11+ years of hands-on experience across the full lifecycle — provisioning, automating, operating, and maintaining enterprise-scale platforms. As Principal Consultant at Genpact, I lead DevOps engineering that turns fragile, manual delivery into reliable, repeatable, code-driven automation.

My core strength is infrastructure creation and maintenance at scale: provisioning cloud environments with Terraform and Ansible, orchestrating workloads on Docker and Kubernetes (EKS/ECS), and engineering reusable GitLab CI/CD and Jenkins pipeline frameworks. I've automated delivery for AWS Lambda, ECS/EKS, Glue, AppSync, S3 promotions, and Airflow DAG workflows, plus Azure API Management and self-hosted gateways.

I live by DevOps methodologies — Infrastructure as Code, continuous integration and delivery, GitOps-style workflows, automated disaster recovery, and proactive monitoring & observability. From Day-0 build to Day-2 operations & maintenance, I keep platforms resilient, observable, and secure — accelerating it all with AI-assisted tooling like GitHub Copilot.
